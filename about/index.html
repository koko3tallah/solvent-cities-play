Start with one country. Light up the rest.

Glowing Atlas is a geography puzzle on a real globe. Plan short routes across real borders, finish inside the fuel, and spend what you earn on the next country.

Play free

In your browser, phone or laptop. No account, no download.

Every country you light, you got to.

Routes are short: a few countries, a few minutes. This is the first one, from Egypt to France, in the real game.

  1. Pick your way

    Start in one country, visit the stop, finish in another. Each crossing costs fuel, and no country twice.

  2. Finish with fuel to spare

    The cheapest finish earns three medals and the most coins. Undo is free, hints are free, and you can try again as often as you like.

  3. Claim the next country

    Coins buy countries. Finish a trio for a bonus, raise landmarks along the way, and watch the globe fill in.

One journey a day, the same for everyone.

Each day at midnight UTC brings a new route with its own rules, then five countries to name from compass clues. Copy your result and it travels as squares: green up to the best fuel, yellow past it, nothing about the route itself.

Miss a day and nothing is lost. There are no streaks to keep alive.

More about the daily journey

A passport that fills in as you play.

Your rank, your countries on two hemispheres, your landmarks, postcards and seals. Save it as an image whenever you like; nothing is posted for you.
